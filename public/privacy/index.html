Product policy

Privacy

Your packet

Your packet is stored in this browser’s IndexedDB database. The site sends no packet entries to a server.

You do not need an account. The site has no analytics, trackers, remote fonts, or third-party scripts.

Your exports

Printouts, plain JSON files, and encrypted bundles are created in your browser. They are saved where your browser normally stores downloads.

Your passphrase is not sent anywhere. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Your Plus license

A restored Plus token and its latest result use this browser’s local storage. They are separate from your packet database.

Only the token is sent to Sociobot’s license API. The app checks at most once per day while online.

A returned license is removed from the visible URL after it is saved.

Removing local data

Clearing site data, private browsing, or changing browsers can remove the packet and license. Download a backup if you need one.
