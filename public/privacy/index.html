Privacy

Health Visit Packet is designed so your health information stays on your device. Your packet is stored in this browser’s IndexedDB storage. We do not require an account, receive your packet, run analytics, or load third-party scripts or fonts.

When you choose to download a plain JSON file, print/save a PDF, or download an encrypted bundle, the file is created in your browser and saved where your browser normally saves downloads. An encrypted bundle is protected with a passphrase you choose; we cannot recover it.

If you restore an existing Plus purchase, its license token and last verification result are stored in this browser’s local storage. The token—not your packet or health entries—is sent to Sociobot’s license API at most once per day while online. A license returned in the URL is saved and immediately removed from the visible URL.

Clearing browser site data, using private browsing, or changing browsers can remove the local packet and saved license. Export a backup if you need to keep the packet. This site has no server-side health-data retention.
