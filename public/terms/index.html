Terms

Health Visit Packet helps you organize information you choose to bring to a health visit. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency guidance, provider messaging, or record retrieval. Do not use it for emergencies.

You are responsible for reviewing what you enter, keeping source dates and labels accurate, and deciding what to share. Printing, PDF saving, and file downloads may be accessible to others with access to your device or files.

The product is provided as-is. Do not rely on it as the only copy of important health information. Export a backup when appropriate.
